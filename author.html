A little about the author

Faith is the foundation.
Teaching is one expression.

Bradley Hobbs is an educator, Christian author, and lifelong student of Scripture. His life and work bring together teaching, writing, ministry, and a desire to help people grow in understanding, wisdom, and faithfulness to Jesus Christ.

Years spent in education have shaped his belief that meaningful growth requires more than information. People need room to ask questions, reflect honestly, practice what they learn, and be encouraged as they continue developing. That conviction carries into his Bible teaching and writing.

The heart behind the work

Scripture for the life
we are actually living.

Brad writes and teaches with the conviction that God's Word speaks into everyday decisions, relationships, character, responsibility, and the unseen condition of the heart. His goal is not to make his own voice the final authority. He wants to lead readers and students back to Scripture, encourage thoughtful study, and invite a deeper walk with Jesus Christ.

His work explores biblical truth, spiritual growth, discipleship, discernment, forgiveness, church life, healing, and the questions people often carry quietly. Across subjects and formats, the purpose remains consistent: to encourage faith that is lived with sincerity, humility, and conviction.

“The goal is not simply to know more. The goal is to grow in wisdom, walk in truth, and become more faithful in the life God has placed before us.”

The teaching in focus

DISCERNMENT
Men Who Know What Matters

This eight-session teaching grew from a reflection on meaningful Christian conversations and the need for men to look beyond the surface. The inspiration opened a door, while the biblical framework, session content, discussion material, practical applications, and manuscript developed through Bradley's own study, prayer, preparation, and convictions.

The teaching invites men to listen carefully, weigh matters through Scripture, examine their own hearts, pursue wisdom, and strengthen one another through honest and purposeful conversation.

Why teach?

To strengthen men
for the walk ahead.

Bradley hopes these sessions become more than a curriculum completed or a conversation remembered. May each gathering encourage men to grow in spiritual maturity, become more discerning, support one another with integrity, and carry biblical wisdom into their homes, churches, relationships, and daily decisions.

Above all, may the work point beyond the author and the teaching toward Jesus Christ, whose truth remains the foundation and whose example continues to call us to faithful living.

Thank you for being part of the journey.

May the Word of God continue to shape our hearts, our conversations, and the way we live.
