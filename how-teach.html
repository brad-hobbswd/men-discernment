How to Use This Teaching

A shared experience shaped by Scripture, trust, honest conversation, and growth.

The series experience

Walk through the teaching together.

This series is meant to be experienced together. Discussion should remain respectful and confidential. Scripture is the final authority. Men are encouraged to participate honestly without pressure to share matters they are not comfortable discussing publicly.

That is especially important for a teaching on discernment and brotherhood. We want the environment to encourage honesty, trust, spiritual growth, and healthy accountability.

01

Respectful Conversation

Keep discussion respectful, creating room for honest questions and thoughtful listening.

02

Confidentiality

Protect the trust shared within the group. Personal conversations deserve care and discretion.

03

Scripture First

Let the Word of God remain the final authority as men examine truth and grow in discernment.

04

Participation Without Pressure

Invite honesty while respecting each man's choice about what he is comfortable sharing publicly.

The heart of the resource

More than information.
A practice for everyday life.

This has the potential to become a very meaningful men's discipleship resource, particularly because the teaching is not merely about identifying discernment as a spiritual gift or ability. Discernment is taught as a daily practice in how a man walks with God, treats others, processes life, and responds to truth.

Grow in truth.
Walk together.

Let every session create room for wisdom, humility, spiritual maturity, and brotherhood.
