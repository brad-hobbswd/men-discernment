FACILITATOR GUIDE

Men Who Know What Matters

Leader Preparation and Session Support

Preparing to lead honest conversations, biblical reflection, accountability, and brotherhood.

Purpose of this guide

Lead the conversation.
Do not lecture the men.

This guide is designed to help the facilitator prepare and lead each gathering. The men should read their session material before meeting. During the gathering, use the reading as the foundation for conversation, reflection, accountability, and practical application.

Your role is to create a respectful environment where men can speak honestly, listen carefully, examine their own lives, and grow together in biblical wisdom. You are guiding the discussion, not expected to have every answer.

01

Prepare Beforehand

Read the student session, review its Scriptures, and identify the main purpose. Prepare to guide the conversation without simply repeating the lesson.

02

Make Room for Everyone

Invite participation without putting men on the spot. Some may speak readily; others may need time. Respect a man’s choice not to share something personal.

03

Keep It Personal

Guide men toward examining their own priorities, reactions, assumptions, and choices rather than using the gathering to criticize absent people.

04

Protect Trust

Handle personal sharing with discretion. Do not encourage gossip, shame, forced disclosure, or comparisons between participants.

Suggested session flow

A Simple Gathering Structure

Adjust the timing to your group and available time. Keep the focus on meaningful participation rather than covering every question.

5 minutes Welcome and opening prayer

Set a respectful tone and briefly remind everyone of the purpose.

5 minutes Accountability check-in

Invite men to share a brief discovery from the previous session’s assignment.

10 minutes Review the central idea

Ask what stood out from the reading. Reinforce the main point and Scripture foundation.

20 to 25 minutes Guided discussion

Use the questions below. Allow conversation to develop while keeping the focus.

10 minutes Practical application

Introduce the upcoming accountability exercise and ask men to identify a concrete action.

5 minutes Closing prayer

Pray around the session’s focus and encourage men to follow through before the next gathering.

Helpful reminders for the facilitator

  • Use open questions and allow silence. Avoid turning each question into a mini-sermon.
  • Ask, “What did you recognize in yourself?” when discussion moves toward blaming others.
  • Do not require participants to share private details. Personal reflection can remain personal.
  • When disagreement occurs, return to Scripture, humility, and respectful listening.
  • Encourage practical accountability. Ask what a man intends to do, not only what he intends to think.
  • Close the discussion with prayer, encouragement, and a clear next step.

Session-by-session leader notes

Eight Sessions. One Growth Journey.

SESSION 01

What Matters Most?

Help men examine priorities, identify distractions, and recognize whether daily attention reflects spiritual priorities.

Key Scriptures

Philippians 1:9–10 · Matthew 6:33 · Proverbs 4:23 · Hebrews 5:14

Discussion Questions

  1. What stood out to you from the reading?
  2. What tends to consume your attention during an ordinary day?
  3. Have you realized something was receiving more attention than deserved?
  4. What makes spiritual priorities difficult to maintain when life becomes busy?
  5. What adjustment did you make during your accountability assignment?

Accountability Review

Ask men to describe the adjustment they made to redirect their attention. Invite specific examples of what received too much attention and what they intentionally prioritized. Keep the conversation practical and focused on choices each man can make.

Facilitator Guidance

Keep discussion focused on personal examination. Redirect blame toward the participant’s own choices and attention. Encourage concrete adjustments rather than broad statements.

SESSION 02

Discernment Begins Within

Encourage honest self-examination of motives, emotions, reactions, assumptions, and willingness to receive correction.

Key Scriptures

Psalm 139:23–24 · James 1:19–20 · Proverbs 16:2 · Galatians 6:4

Discussion Questions

  1. What did you recognize about your own reaction during the accountability exercise?
  2. What emotions tend to influence the way you interpret other people?
  3. What makes receiving correction difficult?
  4. How can we examine ourselves without falling into condemnation?

Accountability Review

Review the exercise of pausing during a strong reaction. Ask what happened, what emotion or assumption surfaced, and what the participant learned by examining his own response before focusing on someone else.

Facilitator Guidance

Do not let the session become an analysis of someone else’s behavior. Normalize honest reflection while emphasizing restoration, growth, and responsibility.

SESSION 03

Learning to Listen Before Speaking

Practice listening to understand, asking thoughtful questions, and responding with patience rather than rushing to advice or defense.

Key Scriptures

James 1:19 · Proverbs 18:13 · Proverbs 20:5 · Proverbs 15:1

Discussion Questions

  1. What did you learn by listening without interrupting?
  2. What makes you want to respond before someone finishes?
  3. When is advice helpful, and when is presence more helpful?
  4. What question can help us understand rather than assume?

Accountability Review

Review the listening practice. Invite men to share what they learned by allowing another person to finish, asking a thoughtful question, and resisting the urge to advise, defend, or redirect.

Facilitator Guidance

Model listening. Let participants finish. Avoid answering every silence. Remind the group that listening is not the same as agreement.

SESSION 04

Truth, Assumptions, and the Stories We Tell Ourselves

Help men separate confirmed facts from interpretations, assumptions, and missing information.

Key Scriptures

Proverbs 18:17 · John 7:24 · 1 Thessalonians 5:21 · Proverbs 14:15

Discussion Questions

  1. What situation did you examine? What were the facts?
  2. What assumptions did you notice?
  3. What information might still be missing?
  4. How can we seek clarification without accusation?

Accountability Review

Review the facts versus assumptions exercise. Ask participants to distinguish confirmed facts, conclusions they drew, and information they still do not know. Reinforce respectful clarification rather than accusation.

Facilitator Guidance

Use neutral examples. Avoid inviting speculation about absent people. Keep the distinction clear between facts, interpretation, and what remains unknown.

SESSION 05

Brotherhood That Can Handle the Truth

Build a culture of honest, compassionate accountability, humility, restoration, and support.

Key Scriptures

Proverbs 27:5–6 · Galatians 6:1–2 · Ephesians 4:15 · Proverbs 17:17

Discussion Questions

  1. What helps a man feel safe enough to be honest?
  2. How can correction be given with compassion?
  3. What does accountability look like without control or shame?
  4. What did your encouragement or conversation teach you?

Accountability Review

Review the intentional conversation or encouragement. Ask how listening, honesty, encouragement, prayer, or support strengthened trust. Keep sharing voluntary and avoid turning accountability into pressure or control.

Facilitator Guidance

Protect confidentiality. Discourage humiliation, unsolicited fixing, and controlling behavior. Emphasize restoration and gentle support.

SESSION 06

Recognizing What Is Influencing Me

Identify voices, habits, relationships, media, and environments shaping thoughts, attitudes, and decisions.

Key Scriptures

1 John 4:1 · Romans 12:2 · Proverbs 13:20 · Colossians 2:8

Discussion Questions

  1. What influence did you notice during the assignment?
  2. Which influences strengthen your walk with God?
  3. What influence needs a boundary or adjustment?
  4. How do we evaluate advice without becoming suspicious of everyone?

Accountability Review

Review the influence each man identified and the adjustment he made. Invite practical examples while protecting privacy. Discuss how Scripture, wise relationships, habits, and media can shape direction.

Facilitator Guidance

Encourage thoughtful, specific changes. Do not demand disclosure about private relationships or habits. Anchor evaluation in Scripture.

SESSION 07

Wisdom When the Answer Is Not Obvious

Guide men to prayer, biblical principles, wise counsel, patience, and responsible next steps when decisions are difficult.

Key Scriptures

James 1:5 · Proverbs 3:5–6 · Proverbs 15:22 · Isaiah 40:31

Discussion Questions

  1. What decision did you examine?
  2. What facts, options, motives, and emotions did you consider?
  3. Who offers wise counsel in your life?
  4. How do you recognize when patience is needed?

Accountability Review

Review the decision examined through wisdom. Invite participants to describe the process rather than disclose sensitive details: facts, options, Scripture, motives, counsel, prayer, and a responsible next step.

Facilitator Guidance

Do not make decisions for participants. Help them think through biblical principles and responsible next steps. Avoid pressure for details about sensitive decisions.

Review Student Reading ↗
SESSION 08

Becoming Men Who Speak Life

Connect the entire series to words, character, relationships, accountability, and continued growth.

Key Scriptures

Ephesians 4:29 · Colossians 3:12–17 · 2 Timothy 2:24–25 · Proverbs 18:21

Discussion Questions

  1. Which lesson has made the biggest difference in your thinking?
  2. How have your words or responses changed?
  3. What commitment do you want to continue?
  4. Who will encourage you to keep growing?

Accountability Review

Guide a brief look back across all eight sessions. Invite each man to name one lesson, one practice he intends to continue, and a brother who can encourage him. Keep commitments personal and sharing voluntary.

Facilitator Guidance

Celebrate growth without comparing men. Invite a personal commitment. Keep the final sharing voluntary and end by praying for continued maturity and brotherhood.

Our goal is not perfect answers.
Our goal is faithful growth.

As facilitator, lead with humility, patience, biblical conviction, and compassion. Make room for men to learn from one another. Encourage the practice of discernment beyond the gathering and remind each man that spiritual growth continues through daily obedience, honest relationships, prayer, and the Word of God.
