Dedicated to the Men Who Keep Growing This teaching is dedicated to the men who continue pursuing God through every season of life. To the men who carry responsibilities quietly, who face challenges without always knowing how to express what they are experiencing, and who continue seeking the Lord even when the road ahead is unclear. To the fathers who desire to lead their families with wisdom. To the husbands who desire to love with understanding. To the sons who are still learning. To the brothers who stand beside one another. To the men who serve faithfully, pray earnestly, and desire to become more like Jesus Christ. To the men who recognize that strength does not require silence, wisdom does not require pride, and spiritual maturity does not mean having every answer. May God give us the courage to speak truth, the humility to receive correction, the wisdom to recognize what matters, and the love necessary to walk beside one another. May we become men who do more than talk about the Word of God. May we become men whose lives demonstrate that the Word has taken root within us. And above all, this teaching is dedicated to Jesus Christ, the source of all wisdom, the author and finisher of our faith, and the One whose truth continues to transform our hearts. May everything written and taught bring glory to His name.
