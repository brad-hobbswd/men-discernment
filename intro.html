ABOUT DISCERNMENT

Men Who Know What Matters

An Eight-Session Biblical Teaching on Spiritual Discernment, Honest Conversation, Wisdom, and Brotherhood

The Introduction

Some of the most important conversations are the ones we never have.

We spend much of our lives talking. We discuss work, family, responsibilities, relationships, church, finances, frustrations, plans, and the things happening around us. Conversations fill our days, yet some of the most important conversations are often the ones we never have.

There are things men carry quietly. Questions we do not know how to ask. Decisions we struggle to make. Situations we replay in our minds. Relationships we wish were different. Moments when we recognize something is wrong, yet cannot quite identify what we are seeing. There are times when we know we need wisdom, but we are uncertain where to begin.

Discernment becomes especially important during those moments.

◈
01 · Understanding

More Than Recognizing Right and Wrong

Discernment involves understanding, examining, weighing, and responding according to the wisdom of God. Spiritual maturity requires more than reacting to what we feel, what we hear, or what appears obvious. We must learn to bring our thoughts, motives, circumstances, and conversations before the Lord.

✦
02 · Growth

Wisdom Is Developed

Wisdom is something we pursue, understanding is something we seek, and spiritual maturity develops as we exercise our ability to distinguish between what is right and wrong. These abilities grow through prayer, Scripture, obedience, humility, experience, and a willingness to learn.

“

Are we taking the time to talk about the things that truly matter?

The question at the heart of this teaching

Honest conversations. Meaningful growth.

Our conversations can reveal much about our spiritual condition. The subjects we avoid, the truths we resist, the assumptions we make, and the words we choose can reveal areas where God desires to work within us. Sometimes the most meaningful growth begins when we are willing to stop pretending, begin listening, and allow an honest conversation to take place.

Throughout these eight sessions, we will examine discernment from a biblical perspective. We will consider the importance of understanding our own hearts, listening carefully, distinguishing truth from assumption, recognizing influences, receiving godly counsel, making wise decisions, and speaking in ways that strengthen others.

✧

A journey of humility, not criticism

This is not an invitation to become suspicious of everyone around us. Nor is this a study designed to encourage criticism, judgmental attitudes, or the constant examination of other people's failures. Biblical discernment begins with a willingness to let God examine us.

Come willing to listen.
Come willing to grow.

The journey ahead is intended to bring men closer to the Lord, closer to biblical wisdom, and closer to one another through conversations that are honest, meaningful, and spiritually productive.

We do not have to arrive at every gathering with every answer. We simply need to arrive willing to listen, willing to learn, willing to grow, and willing to allow the Word of God to speak.

The greatest measure of discernment is not how quickly we identify the shortcomings of others. The greater measure is how faithfully we recognize the voice of God, how willingly we obey His Word, and how carefully we walk with those He has placed in our lives.

Let us talk about what matters most.
