Theme Discernment is learning to see beyond what is immediately visible, examine what is happening within our own hearts, recognize truth through the Word of God, and respond with wisdom, humility, and spiritual maturity. This series is built upon the conviction that God desires men who are not simply knowledgeable about Scripture, but who allow Scripture to shape their thinking, their conversations, their decisions, their relationships, and their character. The purpose is not to produce men who believe they possess superior judgment over others. The purpose is to develop men who are teachable, prayerful, spiritually alert, and willing to allow God to correct their understanding. Foundational Scriptures Philippians 1:9–10 Hebrews 5:14 Proverbs 2:1–6 James 1:19–20 Romans 12:2 Ephesians 4:15
