What began with a book about meaningful conversations became a personal burden to create a biblical teaching that calls men toward wisdom, spiritual depth, and what matters most.

Where the spark began

A book opened a door.
A burden began to grow.

There are books that offer information, and there are books that leave you thinking long after you close the cover. Talk About What Matters Most by J. Alasdair Groves challenged the value of conversations that move beyond the surface and engage the matters that shape our lives, our relationships, and our walk with God.

The message caught my attention. Men can spend time together, share stories, laugh, and talk about countless subjects while still leaving the deepest matters untouched. Spiritual growth, character, conviction, responsibility, struggles, faith, and the condition of our hearts deserve room in our conversations.

That thought stayed with me. I began considering what a teaching could look like when men intentionally turn toward the Word of God, examine their lives, listen with purpose, and make room for conversations that sharpen, challenge, encourage, and strengthen.

The book that sparked the idea
The original inspiration

Talk About What Matters Most

A Christian Man's Guide to Forging Friendships

J. Alasdair Groves

The book's call toward honest, Christ-centered conversation and meaningful Christian friendship became a starting point for my own reflection. Its message prompted me to consider how men might come together around Scripture, not simply to talk, but to grow in discernment and live with greater spiritual intentionality.

The moment inspiration became a teaching

I did not set out to reproduce a book.
I began developing a message of my own.

The inspiration became the foundation for a different work. Through Scripture, study, prayer, reflection, and the desire to help men recognize what truly matters, the idea developed into DISCERNMENT: Men Who Know What Matters, an original eight-session biblical teaching.

The book helped spark the question. The teaching grew through my own biblical study, convictions, preparation, and desire to see men strengthen their walk with God. The sessions, manuscript, discussion material, applications, and biblical framework are original to this project.

✦
The burden behind discernment

Not merely talking.
Learning to recognize what matters.

Discernment asks us to look beyond noise, appearances, distractions, and passing concerns. It invites us to weigh our words, examine our motives, listen carefully, pursue truth, and make decisions shaped by the Word of God.

My hope is that men who walk through this teaching will leave with more than notes. May the conversations lead to conviction, the conviction lead to growth, and the growth produce men who know what matters and live accordingly.
