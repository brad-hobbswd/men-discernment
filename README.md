# DISCERNMENT: Men Who Know What Matters

**A biblical teaching resource for Christian men seeking wisdom, spiritual maturity, discernment, and faithful living.**

DISCERNMENT: Men Who Know What Matters is a teaching resource designed to encourage men to examine what shapes their convictions, decisions, relationships, priorities, and walk with God. The material supports personal study, small-group discussion, and church-based teaching.

## About the Study

This resource focuses on the importance of biblical discernment in a world filled with competing voices, distractions, pressure, and uncertainty. The goal is not simply to know more, but to grow in wisdom, recognize what matters, and apply Scripture faithfully in everyday life.

The study is organized into teaching sessions, supporting resources, and facilitator guidance to help individuals and groups engage with the material.
