SESSION SIX · STUDENT READING

Recognizing What Is Influencing Me

Examining the Voices That Shape Our Decisions

Student Reading | Session Six

1 John 4:1, Romans 12:2, Proverbs 13:20, Colossians 2:8

Introduction

Every man is influenced by something. Our relationships, experiences, habits, conversations, media, desires, and circumstances shape the way we think and make decisions.

Some influences strengthen our walk with God. Others gradually pull our attention away from truth, compromise our convictions, or encourage attitudes that do not reflect Christ.

Discernment requires recognizing what is shaping us before those influences begin directing our decisions.

What voices, habits, and influences have been shaping my thinking lately?

1. Not Every Influence Deserves Our Trust

1 John 4:1

Scripture teaches us to examine what we hear rather than accepting every message without discernment.

Advice may sound convincing while leading us away from biblical truth. Popular opinions may encourage decisions that conflict with our convictions. Even someone we respect may offer advice that requires careful examination.

Discernment does not mean becoming suspicious of everyone. Discernment means remaining grounded in Scripture and evaluating what we hear with wisdom.

Before accepting advice or allowing an opinion to influence a decision, consider whether the message aligns with God’s Word and produces spiritual maturity.

2. Our Environment Can Shape Our Thinking

Romans 12:2

The people, conversations, and habits surrounding us can influence our attitudes over time.

Repeated exposure to negativity can make negativity feel normal. Constant criticism can affect how we view others. Unhealthy conversations can influence our language, attitudes, and relationships.

The same principle applies to positive influences. Prayerful relationships, biblical teaching, wise counsel, and encouraging fellowship can strengthen our spiritual direction.

Recognizing our influences allows us to make intentional decisions about what we allow to occupy our thoughts and shape our behavior.

3. Choose Relationships That Encourage Growth

Proverbs 13:20

Relationships influence the way we think, behave, and make decisions.

A wise friend encourages growth, speaks honestly, and helps us remain grounded when difficult circumstances arise. Healthy brotherhood can strengthen our convictions and remind us of our responsibilities before God.

This does not mean surrounding ourselves only with people who agree with us. Growth often requires conversations that challenge our thinking.

The important question is whether our relationships encourage us toward wisdom, spiritual maturity, and faithful living.

Personal Reflection

  • What influences have shaped my thinking recently?
  • Are there habits or conversations affecting my spiritual walk?
  • Do the people closest to me encourage spiritual growth?
  • Is there an influence I need to reconsider or change?

Session Six Accountability

Examine My Influences

Today, identify one influence that has been affecting your thoughts, attitudes, or decisions.

Consider whether the influence is helping you grow spiritually or creating distractions, unhealthy attitudes, or compromises.

Make one intentional adjustment. This may involve changing a habit, limiting a distraction, seeking wiser counsel, strengthening a relationship, or spending more time in Scripture.

Reflect afterward:

What influence did I recognize, and what change did I make?

Come prepared to share one discovery about the influences shaping your life.
