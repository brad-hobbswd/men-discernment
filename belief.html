Denominational and Doctrinal Foundation Apostolic Pentecostal Doctrine This teaching is rooted in the Apostolic doctrine of the Scriptures and the foundational truths of the faith delivered to the Church. The Bible is our authority for doctrine, Christian living, spiritual understanding, and discernment. Our conclusions must be examined through the Word of God rather than personal preference, cultural opinion, emotional reaction, or human tradition. The doctrinal foundation of this series affirms: The absolute authority and inspiration of the Word of God. The oneness of God, revealed in Jesus Christ. The deity of Jesus Christ and the saving power of His name. Repentance, baptism in the name of Jesus Christ, and the infilling of the Holy Ghost according to the biblical pattern of salvation. The importance of holiness, separation unto God, and a life surrendered to Christ. The continuing work of the Holy Ghost in the believer's life, including spiritual wisdom, guidance, conviction, transformation, and empowerment. The importance of prayer, fellowship, accountability, spiritual leadership, and unity within the Body of Christ. Discernment must never become separated from sound doctrine. Spiritual experiences, personal impressions, opinions, and advice must be tested according to Scripture. Our desire is to develop men who are spiritually grounded, doctrinally sound, compassionate toward others, and committed to living according to the truth they profess. This teaching is Apostolic in doctrine, Pentecostal in spiritual emphasis, biblical in authority, and practical in application.
