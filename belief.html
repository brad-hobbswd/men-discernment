Denominational and doctrinal foundation

Apostolic Pentecostal Doctrine

This teaching is rooted in the Apostolic doctrine of the Scriptures and the foundational truths of the faith delivered to the Church.

The Bible is our authority for doctrine, Christian living, spiritual understanding, and discernment. Our conclusions must be examined through the Word of God rather than personal preference, cultural opinion, emotional reaction, or human tradition.

Foundational convictions

What We Affirm

01

The Word of God

The absolute authority and inspiration of the Word of God.

02

The Oneness of God

The oneness of God, revealed in Jesus Christ.

03

Jesus Christ

The deity of Jesus Christ and the saving power of His name.

04

The Biblical Plan of Salvation

Repentance, baptism in the name of Jesus Christ, and the infilling of the Holy Ghost according to the biblical pattern of salvation.

05

A Surrendered Life

The importance of holiness, separation unto God, and a life surrendered to Christ.

06

The Work of the Spirit

The continuing work of the Holy Ghost in the believer's life, including spiritual wisdom, guidance, conviction, transformation, and empowerment.

07

Christian Brotherhood

The importance of prayer, fellowship, accountability, spiritual leadership, and unity within the Body of Christ.

A discernment principle

Experience is examined through Scripture.

Discernment must never become separated from sound doctrine. Spiritual experiences, personal impressions, opinions, and advice must be tested according to Scripture.

Our desire is to develop men who are spiritually grounded, doctrinally sound, compassionate toward others, and committed to living according to the truth they profess.
