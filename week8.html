SESSION EIGHT · STUDENT READING

Becoming Men Who Speak Life

Applying Discernment Through Our Words and Actions

Student Reading | Session Eight

Ephesians 4:29, Colossians 3:12–17, 2 Timothy 2:24–25, Proverbs 18:21

Introduction

Throughout these sessions, we have examined our priorities, motives, reactions, listening habits, assumptions, relationships, influences, and decisions.

Discernment becomes meaningful when those lessons begin changing the way we live.

The words we speak, the way we treat people, the decisions we make, and the responsibilities we carry all reflect what is happening within us. Spiritual maturity is not measured only by what we know. Our growth becomes visible through how we respond to everyday circumstances and people.

What difference is discernment making in the way I live and treat others?

1. Our Words Should Build Others Up

Ephesians 4:29

Our words have the ability to encourage, strengthen, correct, comfort, and influence others. They can also damage relationships when spoken carelessly.

Discernment helps us consider not only whether something is true, but whether our words are necessary, helpful, and communicated with the right spirit.

A man may need to correct someone, address a problem, or speak honestly about a difficult situation. The goal should be to communicate in a way that contributes to growth rather than unnecessary harm.

Before speaking, consider:

  • Is what I am saying truthful?
  • Does this conversation need to happen?
  • Am I speaking with the right motive?
  • Will my words help bring understanding or growth?

A discerning man recognizes that his words carry responsibility.

2. Our Character Should Reflect Christ

Colossians 3:12–17

Spiritual maturity influences how we treat people, especially when circumstances are difficult.

Compassion, humility, patience, forgiveness, and love are not simply qualities we discuss. They become evident through our interactions.

Patience is demonstrated when someone frustrates us. Forgiveness becomes visible when we choose not to continue carrying resentment. Humility is revealed when we acknowledge mistakes. Compassion is expressed when we recognize another person’s struggles.

Discernment helps us respond according to our spiritual convictions rather than allowing circumstances to determine our character.

3. Continue Growing With Accountability

2 Timothy 2:24–25

Growth does not end when a teaching series concludes. Every man continues facing decisions, relationships, challenges, and opportunities requiring wisdom.

Accountability provides encouragement along the way. Having brothers who pray, listen, encourage, and speak honestly can help us remain intentional about our spiritual growth.

The purpose of brotherhood is not to create dependence upon another person. The purpose is to strengthen one another while continuing to grow in our relationship with Jesus Christ.

A man who remains teachable continues learning. He recognizes that spiritual maturity involves ongoing examination, prayer, correction, and faithful application.

Personal Reflection

  • Have my words recently encouraged or discouraged someone?
  • What area of my character needs continued growth?
  • What lesson from these sessions has challenged me most?
  • What change do I want to continue practicing?
  • Who can help hold me accountable as I continue growing?

Session Eight Accountability

My Commitment to Continued Growth

Reflect on all eight sessions.

Identify:

One priority I have adjusted.

One personal pattern I have recognized.

One listening habit I am improving.

One assumption I have learned to examine.

One relationship in which I want to practice greater honesty or compassion.

One influence I have reconsidered.

One decision in which I am seeking greater wisdom.

One commitment I intend to continue beyond this teaching.

Write a brief personal commitment describing how you intend to practice discernment in your everyday life.

Share your commitment with a trusted brother who can encourage you and pray with you.

Conclusion

Discernment is a lifelong practice. We will continue encountering situations requiring patience, wisdom, honesty, humility, and spiritual maturity.

The purpose of these sessions has been to encourage men to examine themselves, listen carefully, recognize truth, seek wisdom, strengthen relationships, and respond in ways that honor God.

We do not have to be perfect to continue growing. We must remain willing to learn, receive correction, and allow the Word of God to shape our lives.

May we become men who recognize what matters, respond with wisdom, speak with grace, walk in truth, and strengthen one another as we follow Jesus Christ.
