Publication About This Teaching Discernment: Men Who Know What Matters is an eight-week biblical teaching designed for men's groups, Bible studies, church fellowship gatherings, discipleship settings, and personal spiritual development. The manuscript includes biblical teaching, Scripture references, discussion material, practical applications, and opportunities for personal reflection and group conversation. The lessons are intended to be taught sequentially, allowing each week's principles to build upon the previous lesson. This material may be used in small-group settings and ministry environments with appropriate acknowledgment of the author. The manuscript is intended to encourage discussion and biblical application rather than replace personal Bible study, pastoral guidance, or the authority of Scripture. Publication Statement Discernment: Men Who Know What Matters An Eight-Week Apostolic Biblical Teaching on Spiritual Discernment Written and developed by Bradley Hobbs Original teaching manuscript. Scripture references are provided throughout the teaching for personal study and group discussion. Readers are encouraged to examine every teaching through the Word of God.
