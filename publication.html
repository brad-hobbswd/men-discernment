About This Teaching

Discernment: Men Who Know What Matters

Discernment: Men Who Know What Matters is an eight-week biblical teaching designed for men's groups, Bible studies, church fellowship gatherings, discipleship settings, and personal spiritual development.

The manuscript includes biblical teaching, Scripture references, discussion material, practical applications, and opportunities for personal reflection and group conversation.

The lessons are intended to be taught sequentially, allowing each week's principles to build upon the previous lesson.

The Theme

Learning to see clearly.
Responding faithfully.

Discernment is learning to see beyond what is immediately visible, examine what is happening within our own hearts, recognize truth through the Word of God, and respond with wisdom, humility, and spiritual maturity.

This series is built upon the conviction that God desires men who are not simply knowledgeable about Scripture, but who allow Scripture to shape their thinking, their conversations, their decisions, their relationships, and their character.

The purpose is not to produce men who believe they possess superior judgment over others. The purpose is to develop men who are teachable, prayerful, spiritually alert, and willing to allow God to correct their understanding.

01

Look Beyond the Surface

Slow down long enough to understand the circumstances, recognize what is influencing a response, and avoid conclusions built only on appearances.

02

Examine the Heart

Discernment begins with our willingness to invite God to search our motives, thoughts, reactions, and priorities.

03

Measure Through Scripture

God's Word remains the foundation for evaluating beliefs, advice, choices, attitudes, and spiritual impressions.

04

Respond With Wisdom

Understanding should lead us toward humility, patience, truth, compassion, obedience, and choices that honor God.

Use of the Manuscript

Study. Discuss. Apply.

This material may be used in small-group settings and ministry environments with appropriate acknowledgment of the author. The manuscript is intended to encourage discussion and biblical application rather than replace personal Bible study, pastoral guidance, or the authority of Scripture.
